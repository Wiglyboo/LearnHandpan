Play real songs on your handpan.

Each song is written as sheet music with the pad number above every note, so you know which pad to hit and with which hand. Press play to hear it, then play along.

Reading the numbers

D is the ding in the centre. Pads 1 to 8 climb from the lowest note to the highest in a zigzag.

Even numbers sit on the left, so your left hand plays them. Odd numbers sit on the right, for your right hand.
