Twinkle, Twinkle Hard

Traditional French tune
hard arrangement for D Kurd 9 handpan

Pad numbers

D D3 ding1 A32 B♭33 C44 D4 5 E46 F47 G48 A4

How to read it

  • All numbers sit above the notes
  • Upper row: left hand, plays the even pads (2 4 6 8)
  • Lower row: right hand, plays the odd pads (1 3 5 7)
  • D is the ding, T is a tak: tap the steel between pads with the hand its row shows. Taks are drawn as ✕ notes
  • Two numbers stacked: both hands at the same time
  • A and B each repeat once, then C ends the song
Ready · A, B twice each, then C

Practice plan

  1. Learn the medium version first. Section A is that tak groove, so it should already be in your hands.
  2. Section B, the runs. The melody stays on each beat. The note in between is the next pad over: 3 steps to 4, 7 steps to 8, 8 steps to 7, and the line going down steps down. Play the beat notes louder. Start at 40 bpm and say only the beat numbers.
  3. Watch the same-hand pairs. In section B, bar 2, the neighbour under 8 meets the long 7, so the right hand plays 7 twice. In bar 6 the neighbour under 5 meets the long 4, so the left hand plays 4 twice.
  4. Section C, the finale. The first two bars are the opening with taks. Then roll down from 8 to 3. The last bar walks 1 3 5 7 with the right hand and lands on the ding with 3. Let that chord ring.
  5. Goal tempo is 76 bpm. Raise the slider by 4 bpm once a section feels clean three times in a row.

Notes on this arrangement

The melody stays on the beat and the music around it gets busier: taks in A, stepwise runs in B, and a short roll in C. Pads 3 to 8 carry the tune. Pad 1, the low A, appears only in the last bar, so the piece still fits a D Kurd 9.
